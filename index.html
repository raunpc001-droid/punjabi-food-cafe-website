Built from wood, turf and a wall of green

Step in off the highway and the room does the rest — a slatted bamboo ceiling overhead, artificial turf underfoot, and a living-green wall behind the corner tables. It's the kind of spot where one table is deep in a book and the next is loud over a shared plate of fries.

The kitchen runs on comfort food: Punjabi mains, tandoori snacks, fried chicken, and a shake-and-mojito counter that does the heavy lifting on a warm afternoon.
